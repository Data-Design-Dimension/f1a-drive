# Research: what drivers say they need from 
	•	Visibility and following. Nina Gademan says viral sim-racing videos paid for karting and led to her manager and sponsors, calling social media the turning point. 
	•	Jessica Edgar admitted she never pushed her social following enough. 
	•	Lia Block says people connect more with drivers than with the motorsport as a whole. Fan data agrees: 40% of Gen Z follow a driver first.  
	•	Show up and be loud. Abbi Pulling says many young fans came to the paddock, and F1 Academy normalizes women as racers. 
	•	Ella Lloyd says they are always inspiring the younger generation. 
	•	Amna Al Qubaisi values media exposure as much as racing support. 
	•	Belonging and inspiration. Autumn Fisher says motorsport is hard and still male-dominated, and wants young women to feel "you deserve to be here".  
	•	Easy-access fans. Paddock access is open: drivers chat, sign, and take selfies with fans. 
	•	Reach is still growing. 23% of F1 fans follow F1 Academy; 42% of women fans do. 

## Gaps in sources
Few drivers say "fans should do X" outright. Needs above are read from their words, and I’m treating them as hypotheses.
